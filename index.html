Research Interests

Organisational Security in the Majority World

How do resource-constrained micro, small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) in the Majority World perceive, prioritise and operationalise security?

This question draws on more than a decade of experience in information security and operations leadership. I want to understand how staffing, budgets and day-to-day operational needs shape which security measures MSMEs can put into practice.

Security & Privacy for Underserved Users

How do underserved users experience security and privacy in the digital systems they must use?

I’m interested in how language barriers, inaccessible interfaces and limited support shape the security and privacy choices people can make. I want to understand what would help people protect their information when they have few alternatives.

MSc Research

GPON Fraud Detection via Real-time OLT Events Processing

MSc dissertation, Strathmore University, 2025. I designed and built a real-time fraud detection prototype for fibre access networks using open-source tools. On a controlled testbed, it detected all tested events across three simulated fraud scenarios involving rogue device registration, service account misuse, and unauthorised bandwidth changes. Mean alert latency across the three scenarios was 2.48 seconds.

Building and testing this prototype shaped my interest in how organisations integrate security controls into day-to-day operations.
